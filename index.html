Fireplace — Privacy policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026 Applies to version 2.1.0 and later (com.kanyasoft.fireplacevVideo).

Fireplace plays the videos and audio packaged inside the app. It works offline and does not require an account.

Data collection and sharing Fireplace does not collect, store on a server, sell, or transmit personal data. It does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting services. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or other personal files. The production Android app does not request Internet access.

Local playback Video decoding and playback take place on your device. Playback state exists in memory while the app runs. The app has no user database, user accounts, or cloud service. There are no server-side user records to retain or delete.

Actions you choose The Share button opens Android's sharing interface with the app's Google Play link. You choose the receiving app and whether to send it. Rate us opens Google Play or your browser. Those external apps and services operate under their own privacy policies. Fireplace does not receive their account details or your review.

Android and Google Play Android, Google Play, and your device manufacturer may process information under their own settings and privacy policies. Fireplace does not embed a separate telemetry service or receive data from a backend of its own.

Children Fireplace does not collect personal information from children or other users. No registration or purchases are offered.

Changes If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with the release that introduces those changes.

Contact For privacy questions, use the developer contact details on Fireplace's Google Play store listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kanyasoft.fireplacevVideo
